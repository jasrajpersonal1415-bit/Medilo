<analysis>**original_problem_statement:**
The user wants to build a closed, throw-away pilot application for a healthcare startup in India called MEDILO for a maximum of 300 users. The application must be auditable, disciplined, and prioritize correctness over speed or aesthetics.

**PRODUCT REQUIREMENTS:**

-   **User Roles**: Customer (mobile-only), Pharmacy Staff (desktop-only), Registered Pharmacist (desktop-only), Medilo Internal Ops (read-only, desktop-only), and a newly added Delivery Partner (mobile-only).
-   **Authentication**:
    -   Customers: Phone number only, no OTP for the pilot.
    -   Other Roles: Email and password.
-   **Medicine Classification**:
    -   **Bucket A (OTC)**: No prescription needed.
    -   **Bucket B (Chronic Schedule H)**: Prescription optional, but pharmacist review is mandatory.
    -   **Bucket C (Schedule H1)**: Prescription upload is mandatory, and pharmacist approval is mandatory.
-   **Core Order Flow**:
    1.  Customer places an order.
    2.  Pharmacist reviews and approves the order.
    3.  Order is assigned to a pharmacy.
    4.  Pharmacy confirms inventory (batch, expiry).
    5.  The system marks the order Ready for Pickup.
    6.  A delivery partner is assigned and completes the delivery.
-   **Pricing**: Pricing is centralized and controlled by MEDILO Ops. Pharmacies cannot set or edit prices. Prices are fetched from a central Price Master.
-   **Invoicing**: Invoices must be auto-generated using the MEDILO-defined price and be downloadable.
-   **Data & Audit Logging**: All critical actions must be logged in an append-only, timestamped log.
-   **EXCLUDED FEATURES**: OTPs, wallets, marketing features, AI auto-approvals, multi-city support.

**User's preferred language**: English
